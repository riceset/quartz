# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors, collaborators, and hiring teams who want to understand Thiago Komeno's background, technical interests, selected work, and writing at a glance.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and digital garden that introduces Thiago as a Japanese-Brazilian software developer, shows the experiences and projects that shape his work, and makes deeper technical writing easy to discover.

## Brand Personality

Precise, warm, and quietly personal. The site should feel thoughtful and technically assured while letting the work and cross-cultural perspective carry the story.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer portfolio templates, loud self-promotion, dense resume walls, decorative card grids, and effects that compete with the content. New work should preserve the site's compact editorial rhythm and understated character.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the personal thread connecting identity, technology, and craft.
- Show real work and specific outcomes instead of relying on broad claims.
- Keep the page calm and readable while giving important projects a clear visual moment.
- Preserve a consistent, handcrafted identity across the wordmark, iconography, copy, and interaction details.
- Make the portfolio useful at a glance and rewarding to explore in depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard-visible interactions, sufficient contrast in light and dark themes, descriptive alternative text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support for all animated elements.
